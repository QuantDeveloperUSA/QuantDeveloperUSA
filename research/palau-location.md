# Palau Country - Location Research

## Overview
Palau (Republic of Palau) is an island country in the **western Pacific Ocean**, in the subregion of **Micronesia**, Oceania.

## Geographic Details
- **Coordinates**: ~7.5°N, 134.5°E
- **Region**: Micronesia, Oceania
- **Ocean**: Philippine Sea / Pacific Ocean
- **Capital**: Ngerulmud (on Babeldaob island)
- **Land area**: ~459 km² (177 sq mi)
- **Archipelago**: Over 500 islands, part of the Caroline Islands chain

## Neighboring Countries
- **West**: Philippines (~800 km east of the Philippines)
- **South**: Indonesia
- **East**: Federated States of Micronesia
- **North**: ~3,200 km south of Tokyo, Japan

## Political Status
- Independent since 1994
- Compact of Free Association with the United States
